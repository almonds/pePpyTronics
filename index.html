How to update

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Turn the console on and plug it into the computer with a USB cable.
  3. Click Update my console below.
  4. Pick the port that appears in the popup, then click Connect.
  5. Wait about a minute. Don't unplug it until it says it's done.
This browser can't talk to the console. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page needs a secure (https) connection to work.

No port shows up?

Try a different USB cable. Some cables only charge and can't send data. If that doesn't help, the computer may need a driver for the console's USB chip: CP210x driver or CH340 driver. Install it, restart the computer, and try again.
